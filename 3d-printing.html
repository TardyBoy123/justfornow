WHAT TO EXPECT

Affordable custom prints,
with realistic expectations.

01

Made to order

Every job is printed for your project. Turnaround depends on size, material, complexity and printer availability.

02

Normal FDM finish

Fine layer lines, small support marks and minor surface imperfections are normal with FDM printing.

03

Small fit variation

Dimensions can vary slightly. Tight-fit or replacement parts may occasionally need a small revision.

04

Material matters

I’ll recommend PLA, PETG or TPU based on heat, flexibility, strength and intended use.

05

As-printed finish

Sanding, painting, polishing, filling and other post-processing are not included unless specifically agreed on.

06

Price first

You’ll receive the price and estimated turnaround before anything is printed, so there are no surprise charges.

This is a budget-friendly service for one-offs, prototypes, replacement parts, hobby projects and small batches—not industrial production or safety-critical manufacturing. I may decline projects that require extreme precision, safety-critical performance or capabilities outside FDM printing.
